# если у вас нету тёти - sergey nikitin
happy new year | 9/25/2026 | 7 min

---

my original unofficial goal with this cover thing was to have a new cover out once a month, with an end goal of 12 songs covered by the end of the year. clearly, that did not happen. this song is the reason why.<sup>1</sup>

not because I took a break or anything. I've been working on it for a while. it's just been giving me a much harder time than the past few I've done.

for those, I've stopped when it starts to feel good for me to listen to. then I'd come back to it a few hours later and see if it still felt good.<sup>2</sup> for some reason, every time I came back to this one, it did not feel good. in fact sometimes I was astonished at how I ever could have been satisfied with the state of it. so I would try to fix it up, completely transform the feel of it in the process, and then repeat when it inevitably did not satisfy. after a few iterations of that, hours between attempts became days and days became weeks and my patience with myself began to run thin.

so I've decided to just let it be. I think there's probably a good chance I'm being harder on myself than I need to and it's better than I think it is. I also need to remind myself I'm still learning and inevitably things won't always come easily. that's ok!

anyways. 

I really do love this song despite my frustration in attempting to cover it. my mom used to play it whenever she got out her guitar at parties, gatherings, camping, etc. and for that reason it is an absolute staple of my childhood. 

she's also basically the reason I got into music and am doing any of this now, so for me learning it felt like an homage to her and a thank you. she doesn't play guitar as much anymore due to some health complications, so getting it right felt important to me. maybe that's also part of the reason why it's taken so long for me to post.

it also just so happens to be in Russian. I'm a heritage speaker (it's the language I use with my family), but I've always been pretty terrible with it. limited vocabulary, poor pronunciation, bad grammar, etc etc. I know just enough to get by with my family, but put me anywhere else and I struggle. but I've been trying to change that lately. I think knowing Russian is one of the coolest things about me and it would be so much cooler if I actually spoke it well. so I'm doing my best. covering this song is one part of that.

also it's just a fun song with a sweet message. it comes from an old soviet holiday movie that my parents used to watch as kids and recently put me on called Ирония судьбы, или С лёгким паром! In English: The Irony of Fate, or Enjoy Your Bath! it's like the most soviet movie of all time. the premise is that a man gets super drunk on new year's eve and accidentally gets on a flight to Leningrad instead of Moscow. but he's so drunk that he doesn't notice and this is all made worse by the fact that the layout of Lengingrad and Moscow is essentially the same (Soviet Union thing according to my dad) so his home address in Moscow leads to an identical apartment building in Leningrad with his exact apartment layout. fun idea, very adam sandler movie vibes but that's kinda what you want for a holiday classic you watch every year. I'm honestly annoyed that my parents didn't put me on it sooner but it's ok better late than never. the message of the song is that living a fulfilling life inherently involves some level of loss and risk. I never understood that as a kid but it finally clicked with me a few years ago and I was like "woah...... that's crazy fr so true"

anyways this is all to say please enjoy the song and I will try to get the next one out before the end of next month but if it doesn't happen that is also ok goodbye enjoy 👊
