# boyfriends
4656 words | 11/11/2026

---

my therapist says I need to lay it all out. that it's not healthy to have kept it to myself for so long. 

I won't pretend to understand it. I have plenty of other problems. why must we keep revisiting this one? 

but I do like to think after 20 years in the business that she knows what she's talking about. so here it is. to the best of my ability, the complete, unabridged story.

<p align="center">* * *</p>

I met him in my freshman year of college. 

it was all still remote that year and I was desperate to meet people, so I joined like 50 clubs. not even stuff that I was particularly interested in. like I tried to rush for a business frat. I have ZERO interest in business. absolutely none. and let me tell you, rushing a business frat as a woman is not fun, and even less so over zoom. just a bunch of terribly awkward, self important dudes stumbling over their words, asking me about my passions. 

needless to say I did not join and it was a mutual decision. 

but there were some good ones. one such club was focused on teaching kids in disadvantaged areas. I had done a lot of teaching in high school and I always really enjoyed it, so I thought it might be a good time. I applied.

luckily for me they take literally everyone, so by the next week I was in my first meeting, along with 30 others, most of whom already knew each other. the guy in charge, Carey, was immediately impressive. he had a certain air about him the way a politician or a celebrity or a famous athlete would. and he knew it. he seemed to be the kind of person that if you made him supreme leader of the world and gave him total control over everything and everyone, he would make it better. at least, to the extent that it's possible for the world to be good for everyone. 

but anyways, this isn't about him, impressive as he was. after going over the goals of the club and listing the schools we were serving, Carey divided us up into teams. these would be the people we worked most closely with in the club. and then he sent us off into breakout rooms to "mingle." which sounded and sounds absolutely terrifying. 

I debated just dropping from the call then and there. but I didn't. I was the last person to join the room. 

I was surprised to see that already they had begun to strike up a lively conversation, mostly driven by one of the guys in the room. he had a nervous energy about him, but he was willing to take the lead when no one else would, and I think people appreciated that. 

he was prompting everyone on their COVID activities. one guy was being taught to crochet by his girlfriend. one girl was learning to cook. another training for a marathon. someone else was learning Spanish. 

the second he saw me join, he instantly brought me into the conversation. 

I was asked what my COVID activity was and I told them I was learning to handstand. this guy was blown away. he asked me a million questions about it, in a way that made it seem he was genuinely interested. 

>*why'd you decide to learn to handstand?*

>*have you ever done gymnastics?*

>*what makes a good handstand?*

>*how long have you been training?*

>*are you ever worried you'll fall?*

>*how long can you hold it?*

...and on, and on. and then he asked me if I would be willing to demonstrate. at this point we had been talking about it for so long, it would have been insane to say no. so I did it. somehow after joining the room 30 seconds late, I had been made the center of attention. he made me the center of attention.

when I finished, he asked me how to say my name and he pronounced his. Andrew. one of those where it's pronounced differently than it's spelled. and then he told me he liked my voice. and then he moved on to the next person. and he made them the center of attention. and so it continued for the rest of the call. 

<p align="center">* * *</p>

we met every Tuesday. it went on exactly like this for a few weeks. Carey would give us his impressive spiel and impress us and then he would send us away to our groups where we would talk about everything and nothing. somehow it always seemed to be Andrew at the center of every conversation. 

one Tuesday was different. he was quieter. lower energy than usual. 

unfortunately by this point the group had grown used to letting him take the lead, so the vibe was completely thrown off and everyone was a bit uncomfortable. after a few failed attempts at starting the conversation without him, we asked if everything was ok.

> *oh yeah, no, don't worry. I'm just having a rough night. sorry guys...*

we reassured him and told him we hoped he felt better soon.

> *I think I should actually get off I feel like I'm bringing everyone down. I'll see you later.*

and he left. we carried on for another few minutes, but at this point there was no recovering. the rest of us dropped not long after.

at this point let me be clear that I had no romantic interest in him. because I'm tired of people telling me I'm not allowed to reach out to someone as a friend. he needed someone. so I texted. that's all it was.

> **me**:

> *Yo*

> *I don’t really know what’s going on but I hope you feel better soon*

> *Here if you need anything*

> **him**:

> *thank u tina*

> *many things are happening*

and that was it. we didn't text again for a while. he seemed more or less back to normal the next week. everyone in the group breathed a collective sigh of relief.

seemingly out of the blue a few weeks later, he texts me at around 10 pm. 

> **him**:

> *hey can u talk*

I was in the habit of going to bed pretty early, so I was already on my way out by this point, but I felt like he wouldn't have texted me if it wasn't important. so I called him.

for the sake of his privacy I'll leave out what he told me, but he was going through something really serious. he had just gotten off a plane to new york for an impromptu trip, where he knew nobody and had booked no hotel and he told me he just needed someone to talk to. so I listened.

the next night he called me again. and again I listened. and again the night after. and every night for the rest of his trip. 

he worked through the difficult stuff within a few days and eventually he was just telling me about his day. and he asked about mine. and he was genuinely interested. it always struck me how good he was at asking questions. I'm not generally the kind of person that opens up easily. not at that time at least. but it felt like he always had the perfect thing to say. he made me feel special. and interesting. and interested.

our conversations got more personal and eventually I was sharing things with him that I never expected to share with anyone. I was comfortable with him. on nights when I wasn't calling him, I was thinking about him. wondering what he did that day. hoping he was doing ok. wondering if he was thinking about me. wishing he would call.

I felt myself adopting his mannerisms. his laugh. the way he texted. his turns of phrase. my mood was tied to his. when he was feeling good, I was feeling good. when he was sad, I was sad. none of this was on purpose. it just happened.

<p align="center">* * *</p>

a few months passed and freshman year ended. the next year rolled around and finally we were allowed to be on campus. we made plans to meet in person for the first time. I didn't want it to feel like a big deal, but secretly I was really nervous. what if it wasn't the same?

the first thing I noticed about him was that he smelled really bad. not like unclean, but a very distinct, sickly, sweet smell. kinda like when you leave an apple in the garbage for too long. I mean that in the nicest way possible. I did my best to look past it. besides that, we pretty much just picked things up exactly how we left them on our last call. I did mention to him that it felt a bit surreal to be meeting him in person, but he didn't seem to hear me. or if he did he pretended he didn't and changed the subject.

he invited me to stay with him at his apartment my first night because the dorms weren't open yet. I started off on an inflatable mattress, and he was on his bed. after talking for a few hours, it became clear that neither of us was interested in going to bed. he joined me on the mattress. that was when I stopped talking. 

nothing happened that night. he didn't make a move and I didn't make a move and we both fell asleep on the mattress, a few inches apart.

<p align="center">* * *</p>

it wasn't long before we made it official. I'm embarrassed to say that I initiated. I told him I was having feelings and that I was scared of ruining our friendship but that I had to tell him. texted him actually. I'll spare you the details, mostly because I hate thinking about how I acted, but I'll say that he was very gracious and calm. and that he was on the same page.

things didn't change much. we treated each other mostly the same. we spent most days apart, but would text intermittently. some nights I would walk down to his neighborhood to see him and we would make dinner. neither of us really knew how to cook, so this usually meant microwaving a frozen meal from Trader Joe's or putting some chicken nuggets in the oven. to this day whenever I walk past his apartment on Seafine I can't help but remember those first few weeks on campus. he hasn't lived there in years, but it still always comes to mind. things were good.

he still had bad days every now and then. when they came, he wasn't shy about sharing how he was feeling. it hurt me a bit to hear he was unhappy. I was happy with him. why wasn't he? was I doing something wrong? he reassured me that how he was feeling had absolutely nothing to do with me and that he was just venting, but it still bothered me. I wanted him to be happy. I did everything I could. 

I listened when he was feeling down. I offered encouragement. I snuck him into the dining halls. I stole utensils for him when he was running low. wrote him nice little notes and drew all over them and hid them with his things for him to stumble upon. I did everything I could to make him feel better, but no matter what I did it was still "one of the worst years of his life."

<p align="center">* * *</p>

things changed when he met Oliver.

Oliver was also in our teaching club, just on a different team. generally, there wasn't much interteam interaction. they ran into each other on the way to one of our meetings, and instantly hit it off.

Andrew told me that he'd never met someone he shared so much in common with before. that talking to Oliver was just easy. the words came naturally. he didn't have to try to think of a million questions to keep the conversation going. it just went. 

as they started hanging out more, Andrew became visibly happier. he would tell me about their nights out with their other friends, and how much fun he was having. we didn't get to spend as many nights together as before, but honestly it was fine with me because he always seemed to be in a better mood when we were together.

from everything I heard, Oliver seemed like a really fun person. I really wanted to meet him. so I asked Andrew to invite him over for dinner one night. and he did.

I opened the door on his fourth knock and Oliver had a huge smile on his face. it quickly faded as he saw me, and he began to squint instead. he was wearing a red knit sweater that had thin, white zigzags on it, oversized jeans, some loafers, and a nice cologne.

> **Oliver**: *oh, hi, sorry, is Andrew here?*

> **me**: *hey!! yeah he's here, it's nice to meet you! I'm tina. come in.*

> **Oliver**: *oh ok. hey tina. thanks.*

when Andrew walked in, Oliver put his huge smile back on. they caught up briefly, and then we got to making dinner. chicken nuggets tonight. we preheated the oven and arranged them evenly on a baking sheet. then we sat down at the kitchen table and talked.

well, actually, they talked. I was acknowledged maybe once or twice, only after interjecting with my own comment. they barely even looked at me. but it seemed like they were having a good time. so I was happy. for them.

when we were done and the two of them were out of things to talk about, Oliver told him he had somewhere to be and had to go. I walked him to the door, said my goodbyes, closed it, and immediately turned back around to Andrew.

> **me:** *wow, he's so nice! that was so fun.*

> **Andrew:** *yeah, he's great, isn't he?*

> **me:** *yeah, I feel like you guys get along so well.*

> **Andrew:** *yeah.*

and we didn't talk again for the rest of the night.

<p align="center">* * *</p>

I began seeing less of Andrew after that. and I definitely never saw Oliver unless I ran into him somewhere. every time I said hi he would squint at me as if unsure of who I was, take a few seconds, and then say "oh hey tina." and then walk away.

when I would see Andrew he seemed happier. at the very least, he had stopped complaining about how miserable he was. but I couldn't help but feel a note of disinterest. like I had outlived my usefulness. I told myself it was temporary and that things would get better. eventually he started doing things that I couldn't look past anymore.

the first was that he started a club with Oliver. he mentioned it offhandedly to me at some point. JEDI, standing for Justice, Equity, Diversity, and Inclusion. apparently they had had a few meetings already. I asked him why he didn't tell me and he said he didn't think I would care. 

> **me:** *oh that's fine, I would love to join though!*

he paused and looked away. after a few seconds:

> **him:** *I'm not sure if that's a good idea. can I think about it?*

at first I thought he was kidding. I was being excluded from the inclusion club, and the irony was certainly not lost on me. but very quickly it became clear that he meant it. I didn't bring it up again, and neither did he. 

<p align="center">* * *</p>

then there was friendsgiving. Andrew invited me through our teaching club group chat (not personally). which is fine. I didn't really care. I mean it was a little weird considering we were dating, but I brushed it off. I figured if I started bringing up every little concern to Andrew, he would start getting annoyed. and he would stop wanting to be with me. I didn't want to let that happen. 

no, I was the cool girlfriend. I was secure, and confident in myself, and I loved Andrew and I knew that everything was going to be ok. and I looked past the things that worried me.

friendsgiving was fun. we talked a lot. I mostly talked to people other than Andrew. in fact, it seemed like he was avoiding me. whenever I tried to walk over to him, he would walk away. we drank a lot. Andrew in particular got super drunk. so drunk that for some reason he felt it was a good idea to "hold feet" with Oliver. and by that I mean they interlocked their toes and took photos and shots and died laughing. I laughed too. it was funny.

eventually we ran out of alcohol and I, along with Oliver were tasked with heading down to Ralph's to get more vodka. on our way out Andrew called out to us.

> **Andrew**: *I LOVE YOU, OLIVER!*

then he paused and looked around and noticed some confused looks.

> **Andrew**: *...and tina. love you tina.*

quite possibly the most embarrassed I have been maybe ever. I think about that moment at night as I'm going to bed and it keeps me from falling asleep. to this day, it hurts. I mean it's hilarious, don't get me wrong. but holy shit it hurt.

<p align="center">* * *</p>

then there was the note.

I was staying the night at Andrew's. he was brushing his teeth and I was laying in bed. I looked over and on the dresser was a note with a polaroid attached to it. under it, a poem written in handwriting I didn't recognize. I wish I could remember the whole thing because it was quite horrible and embarrassing and I would love to air it out here but what stuck out to me most was a bit along the lines of "boyfriends holding each other in the grass."

looking up at the photo, it was of Andrew and Oliver. in the grass. huge smiles. very close to each other. Andrew came out of the bathroom as I was reading the poem, trying to make sense of it. trying my absolute hardest to rationalize it away. *boyfriends* holding each other... what else could that mean? am I a fucking moron? why have I been putting up with this shit for so long. and I lost it. I screamed at Andrew. I told him to explain what the fuck was going on. I couldn't stop myself. I'd been holding myself back for so long and finally it all had to go somewhere.

Andrew was horrified. he almost seemed hurt. he asked me what I was accusing him of. and I said the only thing that came to mind.

he sat down across from me, looked me in the eyes, put his arms on my shoulders and said, "I am not cheating on you. it hurts me to realize you would even consider that possibility. don't you trust me?"

I instantly regretted yelling at him. he seemed so sincere. his pain seemed genuine. how could I do this to him, after everything he's done for me? 

he went on to explain that it was totally platonic, but that they call each other that as a "joke" and it's nothing to be worried about. he said he understands why I would be concerned, but the truth is they're just really good friends. I have nothing to worry about.

I apologized and explained I was just feeling insecure. that I was paranoid. that I had a history of this happening and I hoped to be better.

he said he hoped I could be better, too. and we went to bed as if nothing had happened.

<p align="center">* * *</p>

I couldn't move past it though. every time I looked at Andrew, I saw the note. I heard the poem in my head. I told myself it was nothing and that I needed to move on if I ever wanted things to go back to normal.

for a few months, I never brought up my concerns. believe me, there were many. but I couldn't let myself be that person again. I convinced myself I was crazy and paranoid and that letting Andrew know about my worries would push him away.

I did everything I could to make things better. I couldn't tell my friends about it, because they were also Andrew's friends and I didn't want them to think badly of him. and because I was worried they would think I was crazy. I cried at a minimum once a week, but I always told myself it would get better. that things would change. 

I started to notice my roommate, Claire, and Andrew getting together more. Claire was also in the teaching club. and the inclusivity club that I was excluded from. sometimes she spent the night at his place. Andrew mentioned it to me offhand once. he told me she was going through a tough time, and he was talking her through it. for obvious reasons this was concerning to me. but at this point I could no longer afford to voice my thoughts. I told myself it was fine. that Andrew was a good person and that Claire was a good person and that they would never do anything to hurt me. I never talked to Claire about it, either. and she never brought it up to me.

there were others, too. Andrew had a lot of friends. lots of them would come over for dinner. just the two of them. which is fine, right? two friends can enjoy dinner together and it doesn't mean anything. Andrew would never do anything to hurt me. he loves me.

I also couldn't help but notice how differently he spoke to me as compared to his friends. it was like he was a different person. even with strangers, there was just something different about him. he would smile, and he was chivalrous, and he would open the door for others, and he would say thank you and he would make jokes and if I was less cool and secure than maybe I would think that he was hitting on them but I am not that person. Andrew loves me and he would never do anything to hurt me.

one night I texted Andrew asking if he had my suitcase. I couldn't remember if I had left it at his apartment or not and I couldn't find it in my dorm. I was getting ready for a trip back home the next morning and needed to pack. he texted back instantly and said he had it. at this point it must have been midnight or so.

I told him I was coming to his to get it because I needed to pack and he didn't answer. which was fine, I assumed he wouldn't mind me coming to get it. so I walked down Seafine in the dark as I had done a million times and thought about my first few weeks on campus with him. and I was happy. 

when I got to his, I knocked on the door a few times and then texted to let him know I was there. he didn't answer. after a minute or two of me waiting at the door, I called him. he picked up.


> **him**: *hey, is everything ok?*

> **me**: *yeah, no, everything's fine I'm outside do you have my suitcase*

> **him**: *oh what. what do you mean you're outside. like you're outside the door? or you're outside the gate? why did you come it's so late*

> **me**: *outside the door. I just need my suitcase I have to pack for tomorrow*

> **him**: *why did you come I didn't say you could come*

> **me**: *I'm sorry. I just assumed it would be fine. can you just let me in I just need to grab my suitcase and then I'll leave*

he hangs up. five minutes pass. I text him a few times, but get no answer. I start to get a sinking feeling in my chest. 

eventually he opens the door a crack and steps out. he asks me if I know where the suitcase is. I tell him no but I could probably find it. and I step toward the door to walk inside. 

he cuts me off.

> **me**: *what it'll just be a minute just let me in*

> **him**: *you can't come inside you'll wake my roommate*

> **me**: *bro what since when are you worried about waking your roommate up*

> **him**: *he asked me to be more careful. if you come inside you'll wake him up. you can't come in. if you tell me where the suitcase is I'll find it for you*

> **me**: *can you just let me in why are you being so weird right now*

I push past him and walk into the apartment and he starts freaking out and hyperventilating. there are tears in his eyes.

> **him**: *listen, I'm sorry but you can't come inside. I need you to leave. just trust me ok. I can't wake my roommate up.*

I'm shocked by his reaction. I've never seen him like this before. he's usually pretty calm. something is wrong. I know something is wrong. but what am I supposed to do? barge into his bedroom and get my suitcase? he would never let me forget it. he would say he's hurt that I can't trust him and that he's not sure how we can be in a relationship if we can't trust each other. I can't have him say that. he loves me. he would never do anything to hurt me. 

so I step back out. I apologize for upsetting him. I tell him we'll talk in the morning. and I go home and I lie in bed all night wondering what the hell just happened.

<p align="center">* * *</p>

we move past it. a few months later, I still get worried, but I repeat the words to myself. he loves me. he would never do anything to hurt me. and that makes me feel better.

we still get into fights every now and then but nothing serious. I never bring up what happened that night ever again. I never bring up Oliver. or Claire. or any of the others. I know if I do then I'll lose him.

<p align="center">* * *</p>

just before our first anniversary, Andrew breaks up with me. he explains that he's having a hard time and he's not ready for a real relationship and that he wants to stay friends.

I do not take it well. at first I do. I show my concern for him. I tell him I understand. and that I want him to be happy. and if this is what it takes for him to be happy then I think it's the right move. and that I want to stay friends.

for the next few months, I spiral. all of the moments I repressed come flooding back to me, and I'm angry. I'm not angry at him. I'm angry at myself.

we try to stay friends. we hang out every now and then. but I can't hold my anger back. it's awkward. he leaves. and then we try again. until we don't.

<p align="center">* * *</p>

two years later, I see that him and Oliver have posted a Valentine's Day photo together. when I see it my stomach drops. I feel physically ill. 

and then I tell myself that it's probably just a joke. that Andrew loves me. and he would never do anything to hurt me. 

and I feel better.
